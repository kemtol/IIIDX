## Interpretation

The frozen ARA-state rule validates as a risk/trade-quality filter:

- Positive in all folds.
- Lower MaxDD than full base.
- Much higher net expectancy per trade.

It does not beat full base cumulative return in pre-OOT walk-forward:

- full base: +174.1%, MaxDD -13.8%.
- `single_near_momentum_0_8`: +111.4%, MaxDD -7.4%.
- `single_plus_near_0_3`: +92.6%, MaxDD -7.4%.

So the current decision is objective-dependent:

- choose full base for max return,
- choose single/near veto for lower drawdown and cleaner fillability.
