## Findings

- The model ranking did not change; AUC and best iteration are identical because the ARA columns are policy-only.
- Hard excluding ARA-like names destroys the portfolio.
- Policy-like OOT picks before ARA filter: 264 rows across 96 days.
- Removed ARA-like picks: 83 rows across 62 days.
- Removed ARA-like picks had mean gross +8.04% and mean net +6.27%.
- Kept non-ARA picks had mean gross +0.89% and mean net -0.85%.

## Interpretation

v19d ORB's large PnL is concentrated in names already near ARA by 14:59. This is not label/model leakage, but it is a major execution realism risk: the current backtest assumes market buy execution at entry price even when live fill may be unavailable.

Hard no-ARA is not the right production policy because it removes the edge. The next useful step is to model fillability, not simply exclude:

- fill-probability haircut for ARA-like names,
- liquidity/turnover-aware sizing,
- or order-book/offer availability data if available.
